Welcome to the pilot.

You're one of the first businesses using PromptHall. This page explains exactly what you get, when you'll hear from us, and what to do when something breaks. Keep it; it's short.

What the pilot is

For the length of the pilot, PromptHall watches your website free of charge. No card, no contract, no catch. In return we ask one thing: when you get an email from us, tell us whether it was useful, confusing or late. That feedback is how we're building the service.

When the pilot ends we'll tell you in advance, show you the paid plans, and you decide. Nothing switches on by itself.

What you get

  • YESA check every 5 minutes, around the clock, of your homepage, the pages you told us matter, and up to ten more we find on your site.
  • YESAn email the moment a page breaks — in plain English, with what it means for your business and the technical details for your developer at the bottom.
  • YESAn email when it's fixed, with how long it was down.
  • YESA report every Monday at 8am: speed score, what changed, every outage of the week, total downtime.
  • YESHelp getting it fixed — see below.
  • NOT YETWhatsApp or SMS alerts. Email only during the pilot.
  • NOT YETAlerts for pages that are slow but still working. Those appear in the Monday report.

When you'll hear from us

  • Right away
    Your first report

    About a minute after you sign up. Speed score, pages checked, what to fix.

  • Within 5–7 min
    "Issue detected"

    A page fails a check, we check again 30 seconds later, and if it's still broken we email you. One email per problem — not one every five minutes.

  • Within 5–7 min
    "Issue resolved"

    As soon as the page passes two checks in a row. The email says how long it was down.

  • Monday 8:00am
    Weekly report

    Every Monday, Lagos time, whether or not anything happened.

  • Otherwise
    Silence

    If nothing breaks, you won't hear from us between Mondays. That's the point.

How to get it fixed

Every alert has a grey box at the bottom titled FOR YOUR DEVELOPER. It holds the exact error, when it happened, where to look and a command to reproduce it. Forward the whole email to your developer — that box is usually all they need.

Don't have a developer, or they're not responding? Reply to the alert email with "please fix this" and we'll connect you to one of the developers we work with. During the pilot we'll quote before any work starts, and the first diagnosis is free.

Not sure if something is really broken, or a sentence in an email didn't make sense? Reply and say so — we read every reply.

Request a fix → Or just reply to any PromptHall email.

What we ask of you

  • Add your important pages. Contact, booking, checkout. We check those first and the alert will say "customers can't book" instead of "a page is down".
  • Whitelist monitor@prompthall.space so alerts don't land in spam. In Gmail: open the first report, click the three dots, "Filter messages like this", "Never send to spam".
  • Tell us when we get it wrong. An alert for a site that was fine, an alert that came late, or no alert for a site that was down — reply and tell us the time.
